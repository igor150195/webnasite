About

Nova Assembly

Nova Assembly is a holding of five standout independent game studios: Unfrozen, Sad Cat Studios, VEA Games, Game Garden and Weappy. The Assembly is co-owned and run by its members, and every studio keeps full creative control of its own games.

Its titles include Heroes of Might & Magic: Olden Era, REPLACED, Nikoderiko: The Magical World, This Is the Police, This Is the Police 2, Rebel Cops, Hollywood Animal, The Eternal Life of Goldman and Iratus: Lord of the Dead.
